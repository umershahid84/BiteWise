# Story clips

Put three short clips here to use real footage in the customer tour's "real life" part instead of the illustrations
(`story.html`). All three must be present; remove them to go back to the illustrations.

| File         | Scene                                   | Ideal length |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------|
| `order.mp4`  | Someone ordering food on their phone     | 7+ seconds   |
| `drive.mp4`  | Someone driving (city street, daytime)   | 9+ seconds   |
| `pickup.mp4` | Takeout handed over at a counter         | 9+ seconds   |

Landscape, 1280×720 or larger, H.264 MP4. The clips are not committed (see `.gitignore`); the finished video in
`public/videos/` is. Record each clip's source and licence below.
